About This Course

The Video Editing course at Programmers Lab is designed to provide comprehensive training in the art and techniques of professional video editing for multimedia production. Participants will explore the fundamentals of video editing, starting with an introduction to the significance of video editing in creating compelling visual content. You will gain a deep understanding of video formats, codecs, and get hands-on experience in industry-leading software like Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects. The course blends storytelling concepts with technical production skills to make you a job-ready editor.

Full Syllabus

Course Curriculum

10 comprehensive modules — from interface basics to professional effects and portfolio setup.

01
Module 1: Introduction to Video Editing
  • Role of video editing in multimedia production
  • Understanding resolutions, aspect ratios, and frame rates
  • Video formats and codecs overview
  • Organizing folders and assets import
02
Module 2: Basic Editing Techniques
  • Getting started with the timeline interface
  • Cutting, trimming, splitting, and splicing footage
  • Inserting and overwriting clips
  • Using keyboard shortcuts for fast workflows
03
Module 3: Audio Design & Syncing
  • Importing and syncing external audio with video
  • Sound effects integration and voiceover handling
  • Adjusting audio gains and channel volume levels
  • Basic audio mixing and noise reduction
04
Module 4: Titles, Text & Overlays
  • Creating text layers and lower thirds
  • Formatting typography, alignments, and background shapes
  • Animating text transitions
  • Exporting subtitle files and captions
05
Module 5: Transitions & Speed Ramping
  • Using default and custom video/audio transitions
  • Implementing speed ramping and time-lapse effects
  • Creating slow-motion clips and freeze frames
  • Split-screen and picture-in-picture effects
06
Module 6: Color Correction & Grading
  • Using Lumetri Color panel tools
  • Color correction vs color grading
  • Applying LUTs (Look-Up Tables)
  • White balancing and skin tone matching
07
Module 7: Green Screen & Chroma Keying
  • Setting up chroma key effects
  • Using Ultra Key to remove green screens
  • Cleaning up edges, spill suppression, and matte cleanup
  • Background replacement and composition basics
08
Module 8: Intro to Motion Graphics & After Effects
  • Interface basics of Adobe After Effects
  • Creating composition layers and keyframe animations
  • Animating logo intros and lower thirds
  • Dynamic Link workflow with Premiere Pro
09
Module 9: Exporting & File Optimization
  • Choosing the right export format (H.264, ProRes, etc.)
  • Optimization settings for YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram
  • Video compression techniques
  • Direct uploading workflows
10
Module 10: Genres & Industry Workflows
  • Editing vlogs, music videos, documentaries, and commercials
  • Managing client reviews, feedback, and iterations
  • Collaborating with producers and directors
  • Freelancing setups on Fiverr & Upwork
