Course Description

Quality Assurance (QA) is a systematic process that ensures products, services, or processes meet established standards and fulfill customer expectations. It involves a series of planned activities and procedures designed to identify defects, errors, or deviations and rectify them before the final deliverable reaches the end-users. Through thorough testing, verification, and validation, QA aims to guarantee that the output is of the highest possible quality and complies with the specified requirements. By implementing QA practices, organizations can enhance customer satisfaction, build trust, and improve overall efficiency in their operations, ultimately leading to sustainable success and growth
