About This Course

The Basic IT Course at Programmers Lab provides comprehensive training in fundamental computer skills and essential software applications. Participants will learn about computer hardware components, operating systems, internet browsing, email management, and productivity software like Microsoft Office and Google Suite. The course also covers troubleshooting techniques, computer security, data privacy best practices, and an introduction to programming concepts. Additionally, participants gain hands-on experience in file management, cloud computing, online tools and digital skills. By the end of the course, participants will have a solid foundation in IT and be able to navigate the digital world with confidence.
